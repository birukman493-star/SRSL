Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I register my school?

Simply click on the "Register" button and fill out the school registration form. Our team will review your application and get back to you within 24-48 hours.

What are the pricing plans?

We offer flexible pricing plans based on school size and requirements. Contact our sales team for a customized quote that fits your needs.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade encryption and security measures to protect your data. All information is stored in secure, SOC 2 compliant data centers.

Do you provide training?

Yes! We provide comprehensive onboarding training for all staff members, including video tutorials, documentation, and live support sessions.

Can I integrate with other systems?

SRSL offers robust API integration capabilities. We can connect with your existing LMS, SIS, and other educational tools.

What support do you offer?

We provide 24/7 email support, business hours phone support, and a comprehensive knowledge base with video tutorials and documentation.
