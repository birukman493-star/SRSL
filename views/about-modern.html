About Abdigudina

A school community built around every learner.

Get to know the people, purpose, and shared commitment behind a more connected school experience.

Our community, our purpose Every day is a chance to help a learner thrive.
What guides us

Great learning happens when a whole community is connected.

Abdigudina helps schools bring everyday learning and essential school services into one clear, supportive experience.

We believe technology should give teachers more room to teach, help families stay involved, and make it easier for every student to keep moving forward.

Our Mission

To make school life easier to manage and learning easier to support through thoughtful, accessible technology.

Our Vision

A connected school community where every learner has the encouragement and tools to reach their potential.

Our Values

  • Students at the center
  • Families and educators working together
  • Useful, responsible innovation
